01WHY DUMUZAS

A good roof does more than cover a house. It helps make every room beneath it feel like home.

Dumuzas is a coated steel roofing sheet designed for reliable performance. Its steel base is protected by a metallic coating and a thin organic topcoat, helping guard against corrosion and surface scratches.

With heat-reflective properties and a versatile profile, it brings a considered balance of protection, comfort and style to your project.

01Features & benefits

Dumuzas combines a strong steel core with a protective metallic layer and organic topcoat. The coated surface is designed to resist corrosion and scratches, while its heat-reflective properties can help improve comfort indoors.

  • Steel core for dependable strength
  • Protective coating system
  • Suitable for roofing, walling and interior use
